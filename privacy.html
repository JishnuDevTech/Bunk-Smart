Privacy Policy

How Bunk Smart collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the service.

Effective date: September 10, 2026 · Version 1.0

Information we collect

When you create an account, we may receive your name, email address, authentication provider identifier, and profile image from Firebase Authentication. When you use Bunk Smart, you may create attendance records, subjects, timetable entries, holiday notes, settings, and challenge progress.

Optional features may receive information you choose to provide, such as location details for holiday suggestions, an uploaded college calendar, browser notification permission, or read-only Google Calendar events after you authorize that connection.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and provide your personal dashboard.
  • To save and calculate attendance, subject, timetable, and challenge data.
  • To show optional holiday recommendations and reminders you enable.
  • To maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
Bunk Smart does not sell personal information and does not mark recommended holidays automatically without your confirmation.

Storage and security

Account and application data are stored using Firebase services configured for Bunk Smart. Google Calendar access, when enabled, is read-only and handled through secure OAuth infrastructure. OAuth secrets are stored as private deployment environment variables and are not placed in the frontend or repository.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your account credentials and signing out of shared devices.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or clear attendance data, export your data, disconnect optional calendar access, disable reminders, and delete your account by contacting the owner. You can withdraw Google Calendar access from your Google Account security settings.

Browser geolocation, notifications, and calendar integrations are optional. Refusing them does not prevent core attendance tracking.
